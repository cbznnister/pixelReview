About Pixel Forge

Pixel Forge is a home for players who love discovering what makes a game special. We look beyond the latest headlines to celebrate clever design, memorable worlds, and the little details that keep us playing.

Our reviews are written for curious players. Whether a game is a pixel-art platformer or a sprawling new adventure, we take the time to explore how it feels to play and who might enjoy it most.

We believe great games can come from anywhere and belong to everyone. Pixel Forge is here to share honest opinions, revisit old favorites, and help you find your next great game.
